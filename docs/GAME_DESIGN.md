# Hollow Canticle

Working title. An original world about an oathbound pilgrim descending into abandoned
places where bells have lost their voices. The reference informed mood, composition,
and camera behavior, rather than supplying assets.

## Pillars

- Readable pressure: the enemy raises a weapon, commits to an arc, then recovers.
- Defense creates offense: perfect deflections fill guard damage and Focus; counters
  suppress recovery. Passive waiting gives an opponent room to breathe.
- Choices with rhythm: a mandatory combat spine, optional reward loops, healing, and
  secrets separate fights. Every third floor tests the player against the Unrung Saint.
- Quiet presentation: cool masonry, warm light, original silhouettes, and a compact
  HUD. Combat moves the camera toward the space between the combatants.

## Combat vocabulary

Vitality is life; guard is posture damage accumulated toward a break. Deflections
damage guard, perfect deflections damage it heavily, and attacks pressure it.
An attack soon after a deflection becomes a counter. At 68 Focus, staying in close
range continuously opens counter opportunities. At 100, Q releases Resonance.
Taking a hit removes momentum. A broken guard gives five seconds to approach and
execute with E or J. Otherwise the enemy recovers. Finishers vary by weapon, enemy,
and the preceding perfect deflection. A guardian's first guard execution triggers
its second phase; the final execution releases it.

Sweeps, grabs, and heavy smashes require evasion. A greatblade can perfectly turn a
smash. Attack arcs, raised poses, directional thrust markers, icons, and audio
provide redundant telegraphs. Enemies have authored combinations and recovery
windows; one attack token keeps groups from becoming simultaneous unavoidable hits.

## Progression

Each clear offers a deterministic selection of three unowned relics. Relics attach
to combat events, affecting defensive windows, wards, Focus, counters, healing,
burning posture, shockwaves, and finishers. Embers kept at the end of a run unlock
alternative starting weapons. Unlocks add choices rather than permanent damage.
Relic discoveries are remembered in the Chronicle. Active runs themselves are not saved.

## Balance limits

Enemy vitality does not scale with depth. Difficulty adds compatible enemies,
modifiers, bounded timing acceleration, and biome mutations. There are at most two
enemies per generated normal encounter. Default timing is demanding; parry
assistance widens it. Safe training prevents death and respawns an opponent until
the six practical milestones are complete.
