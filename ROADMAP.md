# Next releases

## v0.2.1

- Human playtesting of close-view readability, target framing, and camera-relative evades.
- Broader integrated-GPU and browser profiles; tune quality presets against measurements.
- Refine hand/weapon contact poses, character proportions, cloth, and foreground cutouts.
- Expand continuous bridge crossing and environmental interaction details.

## v0.3.0

- More distinctive architectural grammars and biome-specific room objectives.
- Artist-authored character meshes/animations to approach the reference's character fidelity.
- Ranged support enemies and richer group encounter choreography.
- Additional guardian mechanics, weapon exchanges, and relic combinations.
- Controller support and rebindable inputs; evaluate mid-run saves separately.

The current game uses 2D navigation and combat, visual elevation, procedural low-poly
actors, approximate water reflections, and generated audio. It has no accounts,
telemetry, multiplayer, controller/touch gameplay, or active-run resume.
